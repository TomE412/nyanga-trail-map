# Field test checklist

Two tests to do on real phones before race day. Do both on **at least two
phones**, ideally one iPhone and one Android.

## 1. Airplane-mode test

| # | Step | Expected | ✓ |

## 2. GPS alignment check (does the dot sit on the right spot of the race map?)

Pick at least **five places you can recognise on the race map**: a bridge, a
path junction, a building, a summit cairn or a stream crossing. Include
one near the middle of the map and some near each edge if you can reach them.

At each place:

1. Stand exactly on the feature and wait about 30 seconds for GPS to settle.
2. Turn on ☰ › **Debug information**.
3. Write down the values below.
4. Look at the dot: how far is it from the feature on the map, and in which direction?

**How to read the results**

- **Error smaller than the ± figure:** fine. That's normal GPS error.
- **Error the same size and direction everywhere, on both phones:** the race
  map itself is slightly shifted. Send the table back; the map can be
  corrected.
- **Error large in one area only, or different on each phone:** that's GPS
  (cliffs, deep valleys, trees). The map is fine.
- **Error grows towards one edge:** the map may be slightly stretched. Send
  the table back.
